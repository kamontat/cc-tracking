New purchase list: build it as you go

Chosen 2026-11-11: option C. Changes after this mockup: the history is per statement cycle (what each card spent on each of its last 12 statements), not per calendar month. The sheet's picker keeps search and sorting only: Room, Least used, Longest unused, Close date, Due date. Today's Kept in and Account holder filters and the grouping go. Back closes the sheet, as on /pay.

Today a list starts with ticking every card on /list/new, then entering amounts on ?card=…. Adding a card later is a small + Add another card at the bottom of that step, with no room or history to go on. Each option below folds picking and entering into one page, so you can add a card, type its amount, add another, and change an amount, in any order. Every option keeps more than one order per card (DD-186): each order has its own amount and note, and counts read N cards · M orders. Every card also shows its last 12 months of purchases: a small bar per month (this month faded, since it isn't over), the 12-month total and when it was last used. The data is made up, and the options share it, so a list you build in one shows in the others.
