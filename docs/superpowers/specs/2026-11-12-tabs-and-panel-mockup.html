Tabs and a detail panel

Home, Transactions and Cards show one tab at a time on every screen. A card, a limit group or a list opens beside its list from 900px and in a sheet that slides up on a phone. Everything is in the address; ‹ in the address bar is Back. See 2026-11-12-tabs-and-panel-design.md.

Try

Cards

  • Tap a tile: its panel opens. Tap another: the panel swaps, the address is rewritten (Back closes, it does not walk through every card).
  • Pay or Add purchase replaces the panel's content; ‹ in the panel or Back returns to the card.
  • Edit in a card's panel, and + Add card, open the card form in the panel (no form changes the address: it stays ?card=12.3, and Back returns to the card; once a new card is added the address names it). With a form open, tapping another card or × asks first; Back does not.
  • Limits tab: a group's panel lists its cards; tap one to open it. Edit is the group form, in the panel the same way.

Transactions

  • Ledger: a row still opens in place; its Open card opens the card's panel over the ledger.
  • Lists: a list opens in the panel; Continue leaves for the full builder page. + Add list starts a new one (/transactions/lists/new), as + Add card and + Add limit do on their tabs.

Starting a list or a payment

  • No floating + New: things start where they live.
  • A list: + Add list on the Lists tab, or a card's Use on a list. Both open the builder, /transactions/lists/new; + Add a card opens its sheet (add=1, then open=<id>), Add to list writes cards.
  • Save as draft leaves for the Lists tab with the list open in its panel (/transactions/lists?list=…). A draft's Continue and a waiting list's Enter reply open /transactions/lists/edit?list=….
  • A payment: Pay on a bill in Home's Owed, or Pay in a card's panel (a card that owes nothing).

Home

  • Three tabs: Needs you (/) first, Owed (/owed), Ready to use (/ready). Each shows everything, so See more goes.
  • Owed tab: Pay on a tile opens that card's pay form right there; saving returns to the card's panel, which shows what is left.
  • Pay, a card's name, a list row or a ready tile open the panel over Home (/?card=…, /ready?card=…, /?list=…).
