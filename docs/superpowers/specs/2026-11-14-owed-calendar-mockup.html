Owed: money to prepare, by day

Home's Owed tab (/owed) is a calendar of what to pay each day, so the money is ready in time. The same plan reads as a list by week or by month. Today is Sat 14 Nov 2026; the data is made up.

Reading it

  • The control row is the shared one with no sort and no group: search (a card's id, name or last 4) and Filter with its count, then the view switch and a removable chip per filter in use.
  • Filter: Range (7, 14, 30 (default), 60 days, or everything owed), Account holder, Kept in, and Shows (bills, planned payments, open statements).
  • View: Calendar (default), By week or By month. Every view runs in date order.
  • Calendar: a month at a time (‹ ›) with the month's total; a paying day shows its total, darker the more it is, and a dot per card; the last column is each week's total. The day picked (the next paying day at first) shows its cards with Pay, under the month on a phone and beside it on a desktop.
  • By week / By month: one row per day under a heading with that week's or month's total; a row opens in place to its cards with Pay.
  • A planned payment counts on its own date; what it leaves of the bill stays on the due date (7) KBank: ฿3,000 on 16 Nov, ฿3,400 on 18 Nov).
  • Late money is a red banner on top that opens to its cards with Pay; in the calendar, today carries a red ring and the late amount.
  • Open statements are dashed and muted with ~: they can still grow, so they are never in a total.
