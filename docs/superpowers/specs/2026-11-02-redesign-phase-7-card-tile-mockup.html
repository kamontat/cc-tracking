One card tile for Home's Ready to use, the new purchase list and Cards, each drawn inside a copy of the real page at 390 px and 1280 px. A tile is about 60 px tall on Home and 76 px on the list and Cards, so 7 to 9 cards fit on one phone screen. Desktop frames scroll sideways on a small screen.

Tile colour = which limit group the card draws on; cards in one group share it: Family pool Work UOB Empty
Bottom rail = how much of that group's limit is used, the same on every page: under 80% 80% or more over the limit
